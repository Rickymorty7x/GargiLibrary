About GargiLibrary

Public knowledge should be easier to reach.

Our mission is to make PDF documents that are already publicly available on the web easier to discover and access through direct links to their original sources.

Why we exist

Valuable knowledge is often buried inside research repositories, digital archives, institutional websites, and public storage. Conventional search results can place layers of unrelated pages between a reader and the document they need.

GargiLibrary brings relevant PDF results into one focused interface. We show the document title, original host, useful metadata, and a direct source link whenever one is publicly available.

We do not host, copy, unlock, or claim ownership of the documents. We help readers find files exposed by their publishers or custodians, while respecting copyright, licenses, access controls, and provider terms.

Direct by designGo to the original document source with fewer unnecessary steps.
Public sources onlyNo paywall bypassing, credential circumvention, or private-file indexing.
Open and accountableOur source code, methods, limitations, and AI disclosure are public.
Reader focusedA calm interface built for books, papers, reports, and manuals.

Built openly—with AI

GargiLibrary was designed, written, documented, and tested entirely with artificial intelligence using OpenAI Codex under human direction. The project is open source under the MIT License, and responsible contributions are welcome.
